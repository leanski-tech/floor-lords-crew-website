1981 · The Founding Generation

Two Crews Become One

The Floor Lords started in 1981 when two crews came together at an audition in Boston: the New York Puppeteers and the Massachusetts Break Team. They started dancing in the street together, and the Floor Lords were born.

c. 1984 · The Second Chapter

The Family Grows

A second chapter came up from the Ground Master Crew, and the circle widened beyond Boston, with family coming in from Providence, Rhode Island, including Nolee and Archie. Through the lean years the crew held together, and in 1995 Leanski took command and brought the crew back to its essence.

Today · The Ninth Generation

The Family Today

The ninth generation of the crew is led by Lean Rock and El Niño, who were born into the group. The mission has not changed since 1981: use the positive side of hip hop to reach and empower young people, locally and worldwide.
